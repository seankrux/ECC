The 3 rules that never change
Rule 1 · Hub

Each category on the checklist becomes one hub service page in the menu.

Rule 2 · Spoke

Each checked service becomes one spoke page nested under its hub. Skip unchecked ones.

Rule 3 · Offers

The final checklist column is offers/trust — never services. It feeds the Essential Pages tab.

How to use this console
TAB 02 · HUB SERVICES

Categories, services & page outlines

An interactive table of every hub and its checked services. Click a category to open its hub page outline; click any service to open its spoke page outline — H1, title tag, meta, slug, heading structure & body notes, all pre-filled.

  • ✍ Content button — copies a master local-SEO / AEO / GEO prompt. Its output is WordPress-ready: SEO fields (title, meta, slug) split out for the plugin, then the body in clean Markdown that pastes into the editor as blocks — no raw HTML tags.
  • 📷 Photo button — copies a brand-agnostic 4:3 hero-image prompt (no crews, faces, logos, or vehicles) focused on the page's subject and intent.
  • Buttons sit on each row (next to the URL) — copy without even expanding.
  • Every accordion shows H1, SEO title, meta description, slug, headings & schema; Title Case is applied automatically.
TAB 03 · ESSENTIAL PAGES

Offers & supporting pages

The non-service pages every site needs — Home, Areas We Serve, Financing, Offers, Why Choose Us, Contact — each with its own outline. Plus an offers picker: tick what the client offers and it tells you which pages & sections to build.

  • Trust bar / financing / discounts placement
  • Only build what the offers column checks
Quick start (4 steps)
  1. Fill the business bar at the top (name, address, phone, website, city, state).
  2. Open Hub Services → for the current site, keep only the categories & checked services; add or remove to match the sheet.
  3. Build each page in WordPress following its outline. Set the spoke's Parent to its hub so the URL nests.
  4. Open Essential Pages, tick the client's offers, and build the pages it flags.

Your entries are saved in this browser automatically. Use Reset to example in the Hub Services tab to start from the sample list.
