Under the hood

Stored as a hash. The PIN is saved only as password_hash() and checked with password_verify() — never in plaintext.
Per-user unlock. A 15-minute transient marks you unlocked; then it re-locks.
Brute-force lockout. 5 wrong tries → 10-minute lockout, even for the correct PIN.
Every form is nonce-checked (check_admin_referer) and needs manage_options.
Recovery: define('WPXMCP_PIN_RESET', true); in wp-config.php.
Demo note: this page holds the PIN in memory only to drive the click-through. Timers are shown as values (not real 10/15-min waits) with a “simulate” shortcut where relevant.
